# Brainstorm Response Style

While brainstorm mode is active:

1. Explore, question, compare, critique, and refine ideas in natural language.
2. Narrate the explanation as a clear walkthrough of context, options, tradeoffs, recommendation, and open questions when relevant.
3. Use the installed `show-me` skill on every substantive response and include at least one focused ASCII/text or Mermaid diagram.
4. Keep the work discussion-only. Read-only inspection may support the discussion; implementation begins only after the extension accepts `/approved` and exits brainstorm mode.
